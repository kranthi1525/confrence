Manuscript Submission Guidelines

Everything authors need to prepare, format, and submit a manuscript to IBEC 2026.

Before You Submit

Preparing Your Manuscript

Prospective authors are invited to submit full-length original research papers. By submitting a manuscript to IBEC 2026, authors confirm that the work is not substantially similar in content to any paper already published, or under review, at another journal, conference, or workshop during the review period. Papers found in violation will be rejected without further review.

01

Manuscripts should be prepared according to the conference template and should be 3–6 pages, including all text, figures, and references.

02

The manuscript must be submitted as a single file in PDF format only — no other file types will be accepted.

03

Keep the file size under 10 MB, in line with the publisher's submission guidelines.

04

Manuscripts that exceed the 6-page limit may be accepted subject to an additional per-page charge.

05

When converting your source file to PDF, use a tool that fully embeds all fonts used in the document.

06

The final PDF must not contain transparent image layers and must not have any document security or protection features enabled.
