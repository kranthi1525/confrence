Conference Themes

IBEC 2026 invites contributions across six tracks spanning the breadth of bioengineering research.

Theme 01
Biomechanics & Biotransport Engineering

Mechanics of biological tissues and systems, and the transport phenomena that govern physiological function.

↗
Theme 02
Medical Electronics & Instrumentation

Devices, sensors, and signal-processing systems built for diagnosis, monitoring, and clinical care.

↗
Theme 03
Tissue Engineering & Biomaterials

Scaffolds, biomaterials, and engineered tissue constructs designed for repair and regeneration.

↗
Theme 04
Cell & Molecular Engineering

Engineering approaches to cellular and molecular systems, from gene circuits to therapeutic design.

↗
Theme 05
Bioprocess Engineering

Scale-up and optimisation of biological production systems, from fermentation to downstream processing.

↗
Theme 06
Environmental & Plant Biotechnology

Biotechnological approaches to environmental remediation, sustainability, and plant systems.

↗
